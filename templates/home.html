How it works

From report to resolution, in three stages

Every complaint follows the same accountable path, and every step is visible to the people involved.

Report

Describe the problem, choose its ward and street, and mark the exact spot by searching, tapping the map or using your device location. A photo helps the crew find it.

Route

Language processing and a trained classifier choose the responsible department. If it is not confident enough, an administrator decides instead of guessing.

Resolve

The department officer assigns a field worker, who reports the result with a photo. You are notified and confirm the fix, or reopen it if the problem remains.

Who it is for

One platform for everyone who touches a complaint

Citizens
Submit, track and give feedback on complaints.
Department officers
Work a queue filtered by area, ward and street, scoped to one department.
Field workers
See assigned jobs with location and photo, and report the result.
Administrators
Correct routing, retrain the model and watch service levels.

Features

Built for accountable service delivery

Precise locations

Complaints carry a ward, street and map pin, so officers can filter by area and crews can find the spot.

Explainable routing

Administrators can see which words influenced a routing decision, correct it, and retrain the model.

Duplicate checks

Similar complaints are flagged for a person to review. Nothing is merged automatically.

Deadlines and escalation

Each department works to service levels, and overdue complaints can be escalated.

Stay informed

Citizens and staff get notified when a complaint changes hands or status.

Audit trail

Important actions are recorded, so decisions can be reviewed afterwards.
